# Changelog (iPhone and Mac apps)

## 2026-10-10

- **Every displayed sentence is translated.** ~120 Turkish status and error texts that were returned as plain strings (plan and task states, automation timelines, Act errors, file and connection messages) now go through the catalog: 1,408 strings in 6 languages. `contracts/i18n/guard.mjs` fails CI on a Turkish sentence outside the catalog (`verbatim.json` lists the ones that must stay as written); `extract.mjs` adds a build's new keys.
- **Act has a side dock** on the right edge (projects: new, search, import, privacy; a project: preview, code, history, device / full screen / reload, project menu); the bars above and below the content are gone. The writer notice is asked once per kind of project, not with every request. The approval sheet is small (what will happen, İptal / Onayla, longer-lasting choices behind one menu) and the keyboard closes when it opens. The Chat/Act switch has no icons.
- **Voice conversation screen (Chat):** while a voice session is open the chat shows no messages: only the orb and what Wiqo presents (cards, files, inline apps, questions; a step that waits for approval) from this session's replies, newest first; closing the session returns the conversation as it was. Act is unchanged. **Self-hearing fixed:** the microphone is closed to the recognizer while Wiqo speaks and opens 0.45 s after it stops (what was caught in between is dropped), Wiqo yields if the person starts speaking between two sentences, and a sentence that is only Wiqo's own voice coming back is never sent.
- **Composers (Chat and Act):** the field is a Liquid Glass pill of its own, narrow at rest and full width while it is used; "+" and the voice button are separate round glass buttons; the voice button is a waveform glyph; the glass is tinted a little with the theme colour (more while in use).
- **Wiqo answers aloud** (AVSpeechSynthesizer) while a voice session is open: the reply is read sentence by sentence as it is written, in the device language's best installed voice (Turkish otherwise), without markdown, links, code or emoji; a long answer is read for its first part, then "Devamı ekranda."; the person speaking over it or tapping the orb stops it at once (the session uses play-and-record with echo cancellation); a speaker button in the session mutes it and Ayarlar has a switch. The spoken conversation in Chat is therefore two-way and synchronous.
- **Act dock** has no name labels; it can be put away (chevron at its foot or a swipe to the right) and a slim tab on the edge brings it back (tap or swipe left), with a springy slide and icons that pop in one after another; the choice is remembered. The listening orb is softer (gentler deformation, broader highlights) and its glow fades out well inside its view, so no colour edge shows. The chat's suggestion pills are gone (suggestions will come from a cache, relevant to the person, later); "Neler yapabilirim?" is in the + menu.
- **Voice, faster and surer:** a ~1 s fading hum when the microphone opens (Core Haptics; allowed while recording); recognition follows the device language and is given the project's names, file names and command words; short commands run on the phone at once (durdur, geri al, tekrar yap, önizleme / kod / geçmiş, tam ekran, yenile, koyu / açık tema, yazıyı büyüt / küçült); a request that deletes or overwrites is read back and waits for "evet"; waiting sentences are chips that can be dropped; the orb keeps a calm flow while a task runs and flashes when a sentence is handed over; the Chat microphone opens the same session (press and hold dictates as before).
- **Question cards (clarification widget):** a task's question appears as a card in the chat (the question, up to four ready answers, a free-text field). The answer goes back through /chat with the card's `taskId` and `questionId` unchanged, once; only the card in the last message is active (answered, replaced or older cards are read-only; a pending card is active again when the chat is reopened); a Mac step that follows keeps the existing approval and result flow on the same task.
- **Voice commands in Act:** the microphone is a command, not dictation. A real 3D listening orb (Metal, ray-marched, lit like an object, in the accent colour) that behaves like liquid: it squashes and springs back at the start of each word, a ring runs over the surface, the surface and the swirls inside flow faster the louder the voice is, and it sways with it replaces the composer. The session keeps listening while a command is carried out: the sentence is applied the moment the person stops speaking, later sentences wait their turn and start when the job ends, "durdur" stops the job (tap the orb to send now, × to end the session). The Chat/Act switch is Liquid Glass on iOS 26.
- **Act progress is one line:** while the writer works, a single strip above the composer shows the step (Yazılıyor / Telefonda deneniyor / Kaydediliyor, 1/3), three short segments and Durdur; tapping it opens the writer's message. The card that covered the preview is gone; the last-change line is one calm line with Geri al.
- **Chat / Act switch:** slimmer two-way switch with one soft thumb and icons; swipe left/right in the top or bottom band to change side; tapping the current side focuses the composer (Chat) or returns to the project list (Act); a working mark on Act while a job runs and a dot on Chat when a reply finished meanwhile; the app opens on the side it was left on; the sides slide slightly as they cross-fade.
- **Uygulama görünümü** (Ayarlar): theme (system / light / dark), accent colour (6) and text size (small / standard / large). One modifier at the app root (`WiqoAppearanceModifier`), so Chat, Act, the sidebar and every sheet follow; `RovaDesign.ink` is now the app tint.
- **Chat is calmer:** one flat card surface, finished turns hide the plan, task row and duplicate file list under the reply, plan card is one line until opened, copy on long press, empty-chat line and a "do it again" chip for the last PC task, entry motion and a completion haptic.
